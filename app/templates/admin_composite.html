Each award year is one spreadsheet the U.S. Dept. of Education publishes from eZ-Audit, scoring every private nonprofit and for-profit college that takes federal student aid. Load a year and the tracker downloads it, reads it, and attaches each score to the school it belongs to. Nothing is saved until you approve the preview.

Open institution roster

The roster of who is still operating comes from the IPEDS directory (NCES), not from the score files, because the Department does not score public institutions. Refreshing it rewrites the open roster and the sector-size series from the newest IPEDS file on disk.

A school already tracked as closed is never overwritten; the conflict is written to the review file instead.
