Variant 1B, rebuilt for legibility

Montreat College, IRS Form 990, FY2013 to FY2025. Same chart you picked. What changed is everything that stopped you reading it.

What was wrong, and what I did

  • Labels printed on top of each other. Each label sat at its band's midpoint, and in FY2025 the top three bands are thin, so four labels landed within ten pixels and turned to mush. They are laid out now: sorted, pushed apart to a minimum gap, and the overflow pushed back up. Every band carries its name, its dollars, and its share.
  • Five bands, three of them the same blue at different opacities. The palette validator failed that on four checks: every colour read as gray, and the closest pair was ΔE 11.8, below the floor where a reader with full colour vision can tell them apart. Rebuilt as four solid bands that pass. "Other earned" was about 3% of revenue and is folded into auxiliaries: a band too thin to see or name is not more honest than a folded one.
  • You could not tell a surplus from a deficit. For eight of thirteen years the expense line sits within a hair of the stack top, so the one thing the chart exists to answer was the one thing you had to squint at. The strip along the bottom answers it outright, and the line is haloed so it stays visible over a band edge.
