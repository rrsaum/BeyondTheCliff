B · US college enrollment, 1869 to 2023

Students enrolled, not colleges. This is where federal policy shows: the GI Bill lifted enrollment in the late 1940s, and the baby boom with Title IV aid drove the climb from 2.4 million in 1949 to a peak of 21M in 2010. Enrollment has fallen every year since to 2022, then ticked up in 2023. The line stops at the last actual year: NCES's own projection runs modestly upward, not down, so the "cliff" is not yet in the enrollment count. It is a demographic forecast (US births peaked in 2007 and fell about 17 percent), and that pipeline is tracked separately on the Demographic Cliff page.
