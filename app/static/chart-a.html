A · The number of US colleges, 1869 to 2022

Every degree-granting institution in the country, counted each period. The long rise runs from 563 colleges after the Civil War to a peak of 4,726 in 2012; the fall since is what the closure map tracks. The GI Bill (1944) and federal student aid (Title IV, 1965) drove students into these colleges, but the count itself rose steadily rather than in jumps, because those laws filled existing colleges more than they built new ones.
