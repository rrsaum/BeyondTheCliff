All three sectors, back on the map: three mockups

Same data in all three. One hue throughout, on purpose: the brand's three colours sit ΔE 5.1 apart, which the palette validator fails outright, and the dataviz rule for a hard fail is to facet rather than recolour. So identity comes from a label on each panel, never from hue. Hover or tab any dot for its number.
