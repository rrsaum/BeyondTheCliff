Mockup 1, with every revenue source

Montreat College, IRS Form 990, FY2013 to FY2025. Three ways to add the sources to the gap chart. Every band is a figure from the filings, and the bands sum to total revenue exactly. Hover any year for the full breakdown.

Now includes the year you just added (TY2020, fiscal year ending 30 June 2021). Every year in the range is now filed, so no line breaks.

Two things worth knowing before you pick

  1. "Private gifts" here means all giving that is not a government grant, computed as contributions minus government grants. The specific "all other contributions" line in the 990 does not add up: it misses federated campaigns, membership dues and fundraising events, and using it would leave up to $539k a year unexplained inside a stack. This definition reconciles exactly.
  2. Investment income is folded into "other earned". It goes negative in a bad market year (FY2019), and a negative band inverts a stack.

Variant 1A

Earned, then private gifts, then government

The school's own money is the base. Everything above it was given. The expense line crossing the stack is the whole story: where the stack clears the line the year paid for itself, and it is visible at a glance which colour did the clearing.

Strength: three bands is the most a stacked chart reads well with, and the government slab from FY2021 is unmissable. Cost: "earned" hides that it is almost all tuition.

Variant 1B

Every source named

Tuition and fees, auxiliaries, other earned, private gifts, government. The complete picture with nothing folded together.

Strength: answers every "what is in that band" without a hover. Cost: five bands, two of them thin, and three shades of one colour. This is at the limit of what a stack can carry honestly.

Variant 1C

The gap kept as the subject, sources inside it

Closest to the mockup 1 you liked. The earned line is still drawn as a line, so the gap between it and expenses stays the thing you look at, and the bands above it show what filled that gap rather than replacing the idea of it.

Strength: keeps the argument of mockup 1 and adds the sources, with the earned share direct-labelled. Cost: the busiest of the three; the earned line and the top of the earned band are the same edge, which is either elegant or confusing.

Bands sum to total revenue exactly, verified against the filings: contributions plus program service revenue plus investment income plus other revenue equals total revenue in every filed year. Source: IRS Form 990, as filed.
