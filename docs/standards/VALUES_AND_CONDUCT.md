# 360Investor: Values and Code of Conduct

Trust is the whole product. People rely on us to understand the economy and to
make their own decisions about their own money. Lose that trust and we do not
win it back. So these standards are not optional, and they hold for everyone who
builds or writes for 360Investor.

## What we believe

We get the facts right, and we say plainly what we do not yet know. Every claim
names its source. We hold ourselves to account: every call is a dated
probability, and when we are wrong we say so, fast and in the open. We stay
independent and never bend a read to sell a tier. A reader should not be able to
guess our politics. We surface the signals and let you decide. We give
intelligence, not advice. We persuade with evidence, never with pressure or
tricks. We do the thinking so you keep the judgment.

## How we behave

Verify before you publish. Cite the source. Never fabricate a number, a quote, or
a chart. Vet the data before it informs a read, and never call a correlation a
cause. Label a correction a correction. Disclose conflicts and refuse favors.
Keep your politics out of your work and out of public view. Speak up when
something looks wrong, and we will listen.

When members share how they work, the focus stays on process, not performance,
held to the same neutral, civil standard we hold ourselves to.
