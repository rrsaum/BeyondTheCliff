# Beyond the Cliff: the master plan

Status: v1 draft (2026-07-22). The sequencing document. It takes the locked positioning
(`POSITIONING_AND_BRAND_ARCHITECTURE.md`) and the validated model
(`INSTITUTIONAL_HEALTH_METRICS.md`) and turns them into an ordered path from where the work
is today to the endgame: a college or system presidency reached because the body of work made
the case. Owner: R.R. Saum.

Companion documents: `BUSINESS_MODEL.md` (how the practice earns), `BOOK_PROPOSAL.md` (the
trade book), and the candidacy materials named in section 7.

---

## 0. The four decisions this plan is built on

Recorded so the plan can be re-read against them later, and revised if a decision changes.

1. **Product posture: hybrid.** The public surface reports the sector and the closures already
   on the record. Institution-level risk detail is private to that institution and to vetted
   members (systems, foundations, boards). We never publish a living school's survival odds as
   a headline. This is what keeps a president willing to hire the analyst who runs the tracker.
2. **Primary aim: the venture is the path to the presidency.** Formal leadership is meant to
   arrive because the work made Saum part of the environment institutions depend on, not
   because he won an internal ladder. This is the lateral-entry thesis from the late-career
   strategy memos, adopted as the operating goal.
3. **Book: trade.** A nonfiction proposal, a literary agent, a major house. The book is the
   single largest authority multiplier and the flagship credential. See `BOOK_PROPOSAL.md`.
4. **Cadence: nights and weekends.** Roughly 8 to 12 hours a week, no external runway. This is
   the binding constraint, and it is why the horizon is measured in years and the sequence is
   strict. Authority-first at this cadence compounds; it does not sprint.

**The honest consequence of holding all four at once.** Authority-first plus a trade book plus
a 10-hour week is a 30-to-48-month path to a credible presidential candidacy, not an 18-month
one. The plan below is paced to that reality. It front-loads the assets that compound while you
sleep (the book proposal, one flagship report, the model) and defers the assets that consume
hours without compounding (bespoke consulting delivery). Your own rule governs the pace:
**authority precedes activity; revenue must never outpace authority.**

## 1. The mechanism, stated plainly

The whole plan runs one causal chain, borrowed from the late-career strategy memos and made
concrete:

```
  A body of work  ->  institutional gravity  ->  formal leadership
  (book + model +     (systems, boards, and     (a presidency, or the
   flagship report)    funders orbit and cite     senior-strategy and
                       the framework)             advisory rungs on the way)
```

Each arrow is a thing you can cause. The body of work is written. The gravity is earned by the
work being cited and used by people who sit near boards. The leadership offer arrives when a
search committee concludes that Saum already runs part of the environment their institution
depends on, and that hiring him is cheaper than continuing to orbit him.

The interim rungs matter and are not a consolation. A named advisory mandate, a system-office
retainer, a foundation-underwritten cohort, or a chief-strategy role each raises the altitude
of the next conversation. The presidency is the top of a ladder, not a single leap.

## 2. The five workstreams

Everything you do falls into five streams. They are not phases: they run in parallel, at
different intensities over time. The art is knowing which one gets this week's hours.

| # | Workstream | What it produces | Compounds? | Hour cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **W1** | **The Model** | The formalized resilience model and the validated engine behind it | Yes, heavily | Medium, front-loaded |
| **W2** | **The Book** | The trade book: proposal now, manuscript later | Yes, heavily | High, but spread over years |
| **W3** | **The Public Surface** | The tracker, the flagship report, the briefing | Yes | Medium, recurring |
| **W4** | **The Practice** | The productized diagnostic and advisory that earn | Only after authority exists | High per engagement |
| **W5** | **The Candidacy** | The network, the board-adjacency, the materials that convert authority to an offer | Yes, slowly | Low but constant |

The cadence constraint forces a rule: **at most two workstreams are ever the focus at once.**
Trying to run all five kills all five at ten hours a week. Section 4 says which two, when.

## 3. The model, reconciled (W1, and a prerequisite for W2 and W4)

Before the book proposal can be strong and before anything can be sold, the resilience model
has to be one coherent thing. Right now it exists in three overlapping forms that must be
merged:

- **The 2010 taxonomy** (`why_dl_plans_fail`): three themes, Planning and Governance,
  Institutional Processes, Quality Assurance, with a failure-mode list under each, plus two
  structural axes (internal versus external, integrated versus distributed). This is the
  qualitative spine and the prior-art credential.
- **The IVST domains** (the late-career memos): growth logic, ROI and capital discipline,
  regime awareness, integration risk, cost elasticity, governance. Note the unresolved
  five-versus-six-domain inconsistency: the pillar narrative names five, the self-assessment
  instrument names six (it promotes "regime awareness" to a standalone domain). Reconcile to
  one number before publishing either.
- **The validated signals** (`INSTITUTIONAL_HEALTH_METRICS.md`): the out-of-sample workhorses
  are enrollment level, enrollment trend (steep in the final five years), Pell reliance, net
  tuition per student, and the ED composite score and its slope. Holdout AUC lifts from 0.684
  on the federal score alone to 0.736 with your signals added, and to 0.846 on the universal
  roster where net tuition is reported. The admit rate adds essentially nothing, exactly as the
  value-trap logic predicted.

**The synthesis to build:** one model, a small number of named dimensions, where each dimension
carries three things: the boardroom concept a president already thinks in, the observable public
signal where one exists (and an honest "not publicly measurable" where one does not), and the
**irreversibility marker** that says how close a school is to a move it can no longer take back.
Irreversibility is the model's differentiator: viability scores rate a state, the resilience
model rates how much time and how many moves remain. That is what a board actually needs and
what no competitor sells.

This is the highest-value single piece of intellectual work in the plan, because W2 and W4 both
depend on it. It is a design job, not a data job: the panel and the validation already exist.

## 4. The sequence, in four waves

Paced for a 10-hour week. Waves overlap at the edges; the dates are targets, not promises, and
every "done" is verified against a named artifact, not asserted.

### Wave 1 (months 0 to 4): the argument becomes undeniable

Focus: **W1 (the model) and W2 (the book proposal).** Nothing is sold. Nothing is pitched.

- **Formalize the resilience model** (W1). One document, the named dimensions, the three-part
  structure per dimension, the irreversibility markers. Reconcile the 5-versus-6. This is the
  gate for everything after it.
- **Draft the book proposal** (W2). Overview, the argument, chapter architecture, comps, author
  platform, and one sample chapter. See `BOOK_PROPOSAL.md` for the full spec. The sample chapter
  is almost certainly "the graveyard": the $263M-plus of named, dated failures from the 2010
  paper, rebuilt as narrative scenes with the model laid over them.
- **Publish nothing yet.** Wave 1 is heads-down. The one exception: keep the tracker current, so
  the public record stays accurate and the honesty position holds.

Exit test: a literary agent could read the proposal and the sample chapter and understand both
the argument and why this author. The model is one coherent thing a stranger could follow.

### Wave 2 (months 3 to 9): the authority surface goes up

Focus: **W2 (agent search) and W3 (the flagship report).**

- **The flagship report** (W3). One serious, sourced, public analysis, the higher-ed equivalent
  of a definitive white paper. Working title: **The Resilience Report**. It is the argument in
  40 pages with the model and the validated findings, published quietly under the Saum byline,
  no funnel, no sales copy, in the sober register the standards require. It does three jobs at
  once: it is the book's proof of concept, it is the artifact that gets cited, and it is the
  thing you send a system office instead of a pitch.
- **Begin the agent search** (W2). A short, targeted list of literary agents who represent
  serious idea-driven nonfiction (the Gladwell, Christensen, Zemsky neighborhoods). The proposal
  from Wave 1 is the query. See `BOOK_PROPOSAL.md` section on agenting.
- **The briefing begins** (W3, light). A low-frequency, high-density analysis note. Dense but
  infrequent is the rule from the strategy memos: it protects authority and fits the hour budget.

Exit test: the report is published and has been sent, unpitched, to a first handful of
system-office and foundation contacts. At least one agent conversation is live.

### Wave 3 (months 9 to 24): gravity, and the first paid work

Focus: **W4 (the practice) and W5 (the candidacy), with W2 continuing underneath.**

Only now, with a published report and (ideally) a book under contract or in serious agent
conversation, does the practice open. Authority now precedes the activity, as the rule requires.

- **Open the practice, narrowly** (W4). The productized diagnostic first (the paid entry point),
  then interpretation and advisory for the two or three institutions that self-identify. Do not
  build a consultancy. Sell judgment and structure, priced per `BUSINESS_MODEL.md`. At ten hours
  a week you can carry very few engagements: choose the ones that raise altitude, not just
  revenue.
- **Board-adjacency, deliberately** (W5). Convert the report's readers into advisory
  conversations. The strategy memo's rule is binding here: *if your work does not flow to a
  board, it will be ignored.* Target system offices, university foundations, cross-institution
  consortia, and accreditation-adjacent bodies, because those are the rooms adjacent to
  presidencies.
- **Manuscript** (W2). If the book sold, the manuscript is now the long background task, written
  in the same nights-and-weekends cadence over the contract's timeline.

Exit test: at least one paid engagement or funded cohort has closed, at least one standing
advisory relationship near a board exists, and the framework's language has appeared in a
document Saum did not write. That last signal is the one the strategy memos call the real proof
of adoption.

### Wave 4 (months 24 to 48): the offer arrives

Focus: **W5 (the candidacy) and W2 (the book's publication moment).**

- **Publication** (W2). The book ships. This is the single largest authority event in the plan.
  Time the candidacy push to it. See `BOOK_PROPOSAL.md` and the launch note.
- **Convert gravity to candidacy** (W5). By now Saum is cited, used, and board-adjacent. Interim
  formal rungs (a chief-strategy role, an interim vice-presidency, a system-office mandate)
  become available and are worth taking, because each raises the next conversation. The
  presidency conversation opens when a search committee reads the body of work and the candidacy
  materials together and concludes that the analyst who models institutional survival could run
  one. Geography is negotiable by this point, as the strategy memo notes.

There is no exit test here, because this is the goal.

## 5. What gets protected, always

Five standing constraints that no wave is allowed to violate. They are the difference between an
authority practice and a doom-media traffic play, and violating any one closes the presidency
path the whole plan exists to reach.

1. **Never a death-watch.** Reported soberly, framed toward the climb. The standards enforce it;
   the plan depends on it.
2. **Never rate a living named school in public.** Sector and record in public; institution-level
   risk private to the institution. This is the hybrid posture, and it is what lets you sell
   advisory to a president whose school you also track.
3. **Source everything.** The whole asset is trust. One fabricated number ends the practice.
4. **No politics.** The contraction is politically charged; a reader must never be able to read
   Saum's politics off the work.
5. **Revenue never outpaces authority.** No wave sells before the wave that earns the right to.

## 6. The realism section (what could break this, and the honest odds)

- **The cadence is the real risk.** Ten hours a week is enough to build authority slowly; it is
  not enough to also run a consultancy. If revenue pressure or excitement pulls hours into
  bespoke delivery (W4) before the book and report (W2, W3) are done, the compounding assets
  stall and the plan degrades into freelance consulting. Guard W2 and W3's hours in Waves 1 and
  2 absolutely.
- **The agent gate is real.** A trade book is gated by an agent saying yes. If, after a serious
  proposal and a strong sample chapter, no agent bites within Wave 2, the fallback is the
  hybrid-publish path (a sharp book under the existing imprint plus the flagship report doing the
  authority work), not abandoning the book. The authority does not require a Big Five logo; it
  requires the argument in durable form.
- **The presidency is not guaranteed by any of this.** The mechanism raises the probability of an
  offer; it does not manufacture one. The interim rungs (advisory, chief-strategy, interim VP)
  are genuine outcomes in their own right, and each is a better altitude than today. The plan is
  sound even if the top rung never opens.
- **The competitor question.** Anyone could try to copy the framework. The defense is not a
  patent; it is prior art (the 2010 paper), the validated panel most competitors do not hold,
  and the operator credibility a pure analyst cannot match. Keep publishing the proof, because
  the proof is the moat.
